# Privacy Policy for Pendulum Drop

**Effective date:** 8 October 2026
**Last updated:** 8 October 2026

## Who we are

Pendulum Drop ("the Game", "we", "us") is an independently developed Android
game. This policy explains what information is collected when you play, how
it is used, and the choices you have.

For any privacy-related question or request, contact us at:

**Email:** BEHTAREENCODER@GMAIL.COM

---

## Short version

- We do not require an account. You can play the entire game without giving
  us any personal information.
- We do not run servers. Your scores, settings, and daily streak live on your
  device only.
- The game shows ads through Google AdMob. Google collects device and
  advertising information to serve those ads.
- If you are in the EU, UK, or Switzerland, we ask for your consent before
  any personalised advertising is shown.
- If you are in California, you have the right to opt out of the "sharing" of
  your personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising. The
  in-game consent prompt handles this.
- You can stop all collection at any time by uninstalling the game.

---

## 1. Information we collect

We deliberately keep this to the minimum. The categories below are split by
who handles the data.

### 1.1 Information that stays on your device

The game stores the following locally in a file on your device. It is never
transmitted to us or to any third party:

- Your best score, total games played, and perfect-shot count
- Your daily-challenge best score and current daily streak
- Your sound on/off preference
- A counter that tracks whether you have seen the tutorial hint

If you uninstall the game, this file is deleted with it.

### 1.2 Information collected by Google AdMob

The game displays advertising supplied by Google AdMob. To do this, the
Google Mobile Ads SDK collects:

- Your Android Advertising ID (a resettable, user-controlled identifier)
- Device information such as model, operating system version, and language
- Your IP address, which Google uses to infer an approximate (city-level)
  location for ad relevance and fraud prevention
- Information about ad interactions, such as whether an ad was shown,
  clicked, or completed

Google uses this information to serve ads, prevent fraud, and measure ad
performance. It may also use it for its own purposes as described in
Google's privacy policy.

### 1.3 Consent information (EU/UK/Switzerland)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or
Switzerland, we use Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to show you a
consent form before any personalised advertising loads. The form records
your choice, and that choice is stored locally by the Google SDK. You can
change your choice at any time by clearing the app's data in your device
settings and reopening the game.

### 1.4 Google Play Games Services (optional)

If you choose to sign in to Google Play Games from the game's menu, Google
shares your public player display name and player ID with the game so that
your scores can appear on a leaderboard. This is entirely optional. If you
never tap the sign-in button, no Play Games data is exchanged.

### 1.5 Sharing to other apps (optional)

If you use the share button, the game generates a score image on your device
and hands it to the Android share sheet. We do not see which app you share
it to, and the image never passes through us.

### 1.6 Information we do NOT collect

We do not collect or request:

- Your name, email address, postal address, or phone number
- Your precise GPS location
- Access to your camera, microphone, photos, or contacts
- Your browsing history or activity in other apps
- Any information from children under the age of 13

---

## 2. How we use information

We use the small amount of information described above to:

- Show advertisements that fund the game and keep it free
- Remember your scores, settings, and daily streak on your device
- Serve the rewarded video that lets you continue a run
- Show your scores on a leaderboard, if you sign in to Play Games
- Meet our legal and policy obligations in the regions where the game is
  available

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use it for profiling
outside of advertising. We do not use it to contact you.

---

## 3. Legal bases (GDPR, for EU/UK/Swiss users)

Where the General Data Protection Regulation applies, we rely on the
following legal bases:

- **Consent (Article 6(1)(a)):** for personalised advertising and for the
  collection of your advertising ID for ad targeting. You give or refuse
  this consent via the in-game prompt, and you can withdraw it at any time.
- **Legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)):** for fraud prevention, security,
  and ensuring the game functions correctly.
- **Legal obligation (Article 6(1)(c)):** for responding to lawful requests
  from regulators and for retaining records required by tax or consumer law.

## 4. Third parties we share information with

We do not share information with anyone ourselves. However, the SDKs built
into the game share information directly with Google:

| Provider | Purpose | Privacy policy |
|---|---|---|
| Google AdMob | Serving banner and rewarded ads | https://policies.google.com/privacy |
| Google UMP SDK | Gathering consent in the EU/UK | https://policies.google.com/privacy |
| Google Play Games Services | Optional leaderboards | https://policies.google.com/privacy |

Google may share information with its own advertising partners and ad
networks, which are listed in AdMob's published list of certified ad
technology providers. You can find that list inside your Google Ads Settings
at https://adssettings.google.com.

We do not share information with any analytics provider. The game contains
no analytics SDK.

## 5. International transfers

Google is a US-based company with data centres worldwide. When you play,
your information may be transferred to and processed in countries outside
the EU/UK, including the United States. Google relies on the EU-US Data
Privacy Framework and Standard Contractual Clauses to make these transfers
lawful. You can read more in Google's privacy policy.

## 6. How long information is kept

- **Local game data** (scores, settings): stored on your device until you
  uninstall the game or clear its data.
- **AdMob data**: retained by Google according to its own retention schedule,
  typically for a period of 14 months for advertising purposes. See Google's
  privacy policy for specifics.
- **Consent choices**: stored locally by the UMP SDK until you clear the
  app's data.

## 7. Your rights

### 7.1 If you are in the EU, UK, or Switzerland

You have the right to:

- **Access** the personal data held about you
- **Correct** inaccurate personal data
- **Erase** your personal data ("right to be forgotten")
- **Restrict** how your data is used
- **Object** to processing based on legitimate interests
- **Data portability** — receive your data in a machine-readable format
- **Withdraw consent** at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of
  processing done before withdrawal
- **Lodge a complaint** with your national data protection authority

Because we do not hold accounts or run servers, most of these rights are
exercised directly on your device: uninstalling the game deletes all local
data, and resetting your Android Advertising ID removes any link between
past ad activity and you. For requests that require action from Google,
contact Google directly through its privacy tools.

### 7.2 If you are in California

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) as amended by the CPRA, you
have the right to:

- **Know** what categories of personal information are collected, the
  sources, and the purposes
- **Delete** personal information we hold about you
- **Correct** inaccurate personal information
- **Opt out** of the "sale" or "sharing" of your personal information for
  cross-context behavioural advertising
- **Limit** the use of sensitive personal information
- **Non-discrimination** — we will not treat you differently for exercising
  your rights

We do not sell personal information for money. Google AdMob may "share"
information as that term is defined under the CPRA (for cross-context
behavioural advertising). To opt out, either:

- Decline the consent prompt that appears when you first open the game, or
- Reset your Android Advertising ID and disable "Ads personalisation" in
  your device's Google settings, or
- Use the "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" link in the game's
  menu, if available in your version.

Because we do not have accounts, we cannot locate records by name or email.
Requests to delete data held by Google must be sent to Google directly.

### 7.3 If you are elsewhere

If you are in a jurisdiction with its own privacy law (for example, Brazil's
LGPD, Canada's PIPEDA, or Australia's Privacy Act), you have the rights
granted by those laws. Contact us and we will honour them to the extent we
are able, given that we hold no personal data ourselves.

## 8. Children's privacy

The game is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect
personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided
information to the game, please contact us so we can take appropriate
action.

Because the game shows advertising, it is classified for users aged 13 and
above in the Google Play Store. It is not listed in Google Play's Designed
for Families programme, and it does not use ad networks that target
children.

---

## 9. Security

We take the following measures to protect your information:

- The game transmits ad requests only over encrypted HTTPS connections.
- Google's SDKs use industry-standard security practices to protect data
  in transit and at rest.
- We do not store any personal data on our own servers, so there is no
  server-side database to breach.

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee
absolute security, but we limit the amount of data involved so that the
impact of any incident is small.

---

## 10. Advertising ID and how to reset it

Your Android device has an Advertising ID that apps use for ad targeting.
You can:

- **Reset it** at any time from **Settings → Google → Ads** on most Android
  devices
- **Delete it** (opt out of personalised advertising) from the same screen
- **Opt out of interest-based ads** by toggling "Opt out of Ads
  Personalization"

If you delete or reset your Advertising ID, ads will still appear in the
game but they will not be based on your prior activity.
